# CompanySimulator visual checkpoint

CompanySimulator was upgraded from hard-coded baseline, aggressive, conservative, and pivot scenarios with fabricated five-year revenue, user, valuation, growth, churn, ARPU, market-expansion, simulation-count, chart, and strategic-insight claims. Those projections suggested revenue, profitability, sustainability, risk/reward, and a recommended path without company source data, accounting periods, cost structure, customer evidence, valuation method, uncertainty analysis, or audit review. All charts, scenario selectors, projections, and recommendations were removed rather than presenting invented financial or operational outcomes as analysis. No company, user count, customer, revenue, expense, profit, churn, ARPU, market, valuation, return, cash flow, scenario, sensitivity, forecast, or strategic recommendation is read, calculated, charted, exported, cached, or simulated.

Files: `company-simulator-desktop.webp`, `company-simulator-mobile.png`.

The 390px capture was reviewed for readable wrapped simulation-unavailable disclosure, stacked baseline/source, methodology, scenario/uncertainty, and authorization/audit readiness cards, visible finance disclaimer, sensitive-data warning, and absence of horizontal overflow.
