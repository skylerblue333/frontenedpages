# SMSCampaigns visual checkpoint

SMSCampaigns was hardened from an authenticated-looking SMS marketing shell with nonfunctional Sign In, New, Search, Settings, loading, and empty-data controls without a messaging provider or consent-aware campaign service. Those controls were removed. No recipient, phone number, message, campaign, sender identity, delivery state, engagement, revenue, user, tenant, opt-in, opt-out, compliance, or availability state is displayed, calculated, stored, transmitted, verified, granted, or mutated.

The page documents verified provider and sender scope, least-privilege credentials, recipient provenance, explicit jurisdiction-aware consent, suppression and preference controls, content and link review, human approval, quiet hours, rate limits, provider receipts, delivery and opt-out evidence, retention, privacy, auditability, incident response, and communications-law review. Files: `sms-campaigns-desktop.webp`, `sms-campaigns-mobile.png`.
