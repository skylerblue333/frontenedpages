# Arcade visual checkpoint

Arcade was upgraded from fabricated gaming and wagering functionality. The previous screen advertised play-to-earn tokens, PvP, tournaments, quests, ranked competition, and SKY444 wagering for Crash, Slots, Blackjack, Dice, Roulette, and Plinko. It also connected tournament and quest data without a verified game service, player identity, age and jurisdiction controls, wallet, payment or wagering provider, odds and rules, prize ledger, anti-cheat system, or audit contract. Those game links, reward claims, and controls were removed rather than enabling gambling or implying financial outcomes.

Files: `arcade-desktop.webp`, `arcade-mobile.png`.

The 390px capture was reviewed for readable wrapped arcade disclosure, stacked game/account/wager/tournament boundary cards, visible gaming/wallet/age-verification links, responsible-gaming and sensitive-data warning, and absence of horizontal overflow.
