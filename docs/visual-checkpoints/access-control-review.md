# AccessControl visual checkpoint

AccessControl was already hardened to a truthful unavailable authorization boundary. It states that no identity provider, policy decision point, role store, permission-enforcement middleware, approval workflow, or administrative audit trail is connected. No user, service, tenant, session, group, role, authentication context, allow/deny decision, role assignment, permission grant, policy edit, approval, or revocation is displayed or simulated.

Files: `access-control-desktop.webp`, `access-control-mobile.png`.

The 390px capture was reviewed for readable wrapped authorization disclosure, stacked identity/enforcement/change cards, visible security/audit links, server-enforcement warning, and absence of horizontal overflow.
