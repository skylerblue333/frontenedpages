# AgeVerification visual checkpoint

AgeVerification was upgraded from a generic Coming Soon placeholder to a truthful identity-and-age verification readiness boundary. It states that no verification provider, identity match, document review, biometric or liveness check, jurisdiction rule, consent record, content-custodian record, retention policy, or compliance audit contract is connected. The route does not collect identity documents or claim to verify a person’s age, legal compliance, or lawful access.

Files: `age-verification-desktop.webp`, `age-verification-mobile.png`.

The 390px capture was reviewed for readable wrapped verification disclosure, stacked provider/biometric/jurisdiction/compliance cards, visible self-attestation/privacy/contact links, sensitive-data warning, and absence of horizontal overflow.
