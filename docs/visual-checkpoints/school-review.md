# School visual checkpoint

School was already hardened from a hard-coded course marketplace with named instructors, student and review counts, ratings, prices, popularity badges, “Earn While You Learn,” on-chain certificate claims, SKY444 totals, learning tracks, and enrollment prompts. The page now truthfully states that school services are not connected and that no catalog, learner, payment, reward, credential, or content contracts are established.

The page documents course/instructor provenance, curriculum versioning, accessibility, search semantics, learner ownership, progress persistence, assessment and certificate rules, payment and entitlement controls, reward accounting, privacy, moderation, and loading/empty/error/retry states. Files: `school-desktop.webp`, `school-mobile.png`.
