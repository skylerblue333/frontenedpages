# BattlePass visual checkpoint

BattlePass was upgraded from fabricated progression and reward claims. The previous screen claimed a 50-tier Season 1 pass with XP, SKY444 token rewards, exclusive badges, premium status, tier progress, reward claiming, and a Premium purchase path. No authoritative season catalog, player identity, progression ledger, reward inventory, token custody, purchase authorization, age and eligibility controls, entitlement service, or audit contract was connected. Those claims and actions were removed rather than implying that a player earned, owns, purchased, or can claim a reward.

Files: `battle-pass-desktop.webp`, `battle-pass-mobile.png`.

The 390px capture was reviewed for readable wrapped progression disclosure, stacked season/player/reward/audit cards, visible gaming/badge/privacy links, sensitive-data warning, and absence of horizontal overflow.
