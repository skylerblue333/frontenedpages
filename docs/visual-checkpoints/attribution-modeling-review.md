# AttributionModeling visual checkpoint

AttributionModeling was already hardened to a truthful unavailable attribution boundary. The previous screen exposed an authenticated shell with New, Search, Settings, loading, and empty-item behavior, but no verified event stream, identity resolution, consent boundary, attribution model, conversion definition, revenue source, campaign taxonomy, export service, or audit contract was connected. Those controls were removed because the screen cannot establish that a channel or campaign influenced an outcome. No campaign, referral, ad, content, social, search, email, marketplace, or product event is collected or linked to a person; no conversion, revenue, customer, cohort, channel contribution, return, lift, or causal conclusion is calculated; and no identity graph, consent choice, audience, personal profile, exported report, or retention decision is read or changed.

Files: `attribution-modeling-desktop.webp`, `attribution-modeling-mobile.png`.

The 390px capture was reviewed for readable wrapped attribution disclosure, stacked touchpoint/outcome/privacy boundary cards, visible analytics/campaign/privacy/contact links, sensitive-data warning, and absence of horizontal overflow.
