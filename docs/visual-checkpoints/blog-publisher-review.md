# BlogPublisher visual checkpoint

BlogPublisher was already hardened to a truthful unavailable publication boundary. The previous screen exposed an authenticated shell with New, Search, Settings, loading, and empty-item behavior, but no verified content registry, approval workflow, scheduler, channel provider, audience permission, delivery receipt, analytics source, monetization ledger, moderation policy, rights model, or audit contract was connected. Those controls were removed because the screen cannot establish that content is approved, scheduled, published, delivered, or monetized. No article, author, approval, schedule, channel, audience, canonical URL, search index, public publication, email, social post, push, notification, feed update, webhook, delivery receipt, retry, external share, view, click, engagement, subscriber, conversion, revenue, attribution, ranking, or monetization result is displayed, created, calculated, or sent.

Files: `blog-publisher-desktop.webp`, `blog-publisher-mobile.png`.

The 390px capture was reviewed for readable wrapped publication disclosure, stacked publication/distribution/outcome cards, visible editor/calendar/analytics/contact links, sensitive-data warning, and absence of horizontal overflow.
