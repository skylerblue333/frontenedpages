# RetentionEngine visual checkpoint

RetentionEngine was hardened from a fictional growth shell that displayed hardcoded retention percentages, targets, trends, revenue per active user, repeat AI usage, live feature labels, earnings claims, AI outcome claims, user-behavior assertions, and an APY staking share example. Those claims were removed while preserving tabbed governance views.

The current interface documents consent and audience scope, event and metric provenance, reward and finance/crypto boundaries, AI limits, privacy, fairness, safety, accessibility, security, auditability, and least-privilege authorization. Its four views are Daily Loop, Metrics, Feature Governance, and Sharing Safety. They describe what must be verified before launch rather than claiming a live retention engine, user dataset, reward ledger, AI personalization system, or viral analytics feed.

The registered route is `/retention-engine`. No user, cohort, action, retention rate, revenue, reward, APY, AI result, share event, or availability state is displayed, calculated, stored, transmitted, verified, or mutated from this page.
