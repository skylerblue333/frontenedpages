# Reviews visual checkpoint

Reviews was hardened from an authenticated-looking shell with nonfunctional Sign In, New, Search, Settings, loading, and empty-data controls without a review backend. Those controls were removed. The registered route is `/reviews`. No reviewer, author, subject, review, rating, purchase, transaction, moderation decision, user, AI result, safety outcome, or availability state is displayed, calculated, stored, transmitted, verified, or mutated.

The page documents reviewer, author, and subject scope; review and rating provenance; purchase or eligibility context; consent and authenticity; published policy and taxonomy; transparent methodology; human moderation; AI limitations; appeals; corrections; privacy; safety; security; marketplace and financial sensitivity controls; auditability; accessibility; and least-privilege authorization.
