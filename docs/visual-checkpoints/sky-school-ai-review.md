# SkySchoolAI visual checkpoint

SkySchoolAI was hardened from a fabricated AI course marketplace that displayed unsupported instructors, student counts, ratings, durations, prices, an AI-teacher claim, enrollment controls, and personalized skill-tree progress. Those claims and controls were removed.

The page documents verified authored content and asset provenance, model/version and prompt/data governance, grounded answers/evaluation, safety filters/human escalation, authenticated learner ownership, enrollment/progress/assessment sources, price/currency/payment/refund controls, certificate/reward semantics, wallet custody, privacy, retention/deletion, auditability, and honest operational states. Files: `sky-school-ai-desktop.webp`, `sky-school-ai-mobile.png`.
