# AssetManagement visual checkpoint

AssetManagement was already hardened to a truthful unavailable asset-library boundary. The previous screen exposed an authenticated shell with New, Search, Settings, loading, and empty-item behavior, but no verified asset registry, object-storage provider, metadata schema, ownership and rights model, version history, approval workflow, upload validation, delivery service, or audit contract was connected. Those controls were removed because the screen cannot establish that an asset exists, is authorized, or is safely stored. No image, video, audio, document, brand file, marketing asset, metadata, thumbnail, derivative, owner, license, consent, attribution, approval, publication status, usage restriction, upload, processing, conversion, signed URL, delivery, backup, deletion, or external share is displayed or initiated.

Files: `asset-management-desktop.webp`, `asset-management-mobile.png`.

The 390px capture was reviewed for readable wrapped asset-library disclosure, stacked asset/rights/storage boundary cards, visible media/content/privacy/contact links, sensitive-data warning, and absence of horizontal overflow.
