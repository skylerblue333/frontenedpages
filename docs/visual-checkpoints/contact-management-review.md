# ContactManagement visual checkpoint

ContactManagement was upgraded from a fake sign-in gate, New, search, settings, loading, and empty-list controls without an authenticated owner, contact source, consent model, privacy policy, messaging service, or persistence. Those controls were removed rather than implying that contact information can be created, changed, imported, exported, or used. No person, profile, name, email, phone number, address, contact record, search result, consent, tag, list, message, notification, import, export, duplicate, or communication preference is read, stored, displayed, or simulated.

Files: `contact-management-desktop.webp`, `contact-management-mobile.png`.

The 390px capture was reviewed for readable wrapped contact-management-unavailable disclosure, stacked owner/consent, create/import/export, messaging/preferences, and privacy/audit readiness cards, explicit sensitive-data warning, and absence of horizontal overflow.
