# BehavioralIntelligence visual checkpoint

BehavioralIntelligence was upgraded from fabricated behavioral analytics and intervention claims. The previous screen exposed totals, churn risk, predictive accuracy, sentiment, user segments, lifetime value, behavioral factors, and automated retention recommendations. It claimed 15.9K users, 28% average churn risk, 93% prediction accuracy, 7.8/10 sentiment, segment counts and dollar values, 450 at-risk users, and activated retention campaigns. No identity or consent source, data lineage, model registry, validation set, fairness review, privacy basis, access control, intervention service, or audit contract was connected. Those metrics, charts, segment buttons, and recommendations were removed rather than implying surveillance, profiling, prediction, or automated action.

Files: `behavioral-intelligence-desktop.webp`, `behavioral-intelligence-mobile.png`.

The 390px capture was reviewed for readable wrapped behavioral-analytics disclosure, stacked identity/model/intervention/privacy cards, visible audience/churn/privacy links, sensitive-data warning, and absence of horizontal overflow.
