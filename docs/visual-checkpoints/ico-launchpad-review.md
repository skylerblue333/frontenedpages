# ICOLaunchpad visual checkpoint

ICOLaunchpad was rewritten as a truthful crypto-fundraising readiness boundary. The previous screen presented a live pre-sale, fabricated prices, raised amounts, caps, bonuses, participant counts, token supply and allocations, vesting terms, audit claims, payment assets, wallet purchase controls, and sale-stage dates without verified issuer identity, offering documents, contract evidence, eligibility, KYC/AML, securities analysis, payment, custody, settlement, tax, or authorization controls. Those claims and irreversible purchase controls were removed. No token sale or wallet action is initiated from this page. The registered route is `/i-c-o-launchpad`.

No issuer or investor auth check, offering lookup, contract lookup, sale-stage calculation, price, supply, allocation, vesting, bonus, participant, audit score, eligibility check, KYC/AML check, wallet connection, signing, payment, transaction, allocation, refund, API request, database read or write, notification, export, deletion, or personal-data operation is read, calculated, displayed, asserted, initiated, or simulated.

Files: `ico-launchpad-desktop.webp`, `ico-launchpad-mobile.png`.

The 390px capture was reviewed for readable wrapped unavailable disclosure, stacked crypto-fundraising cards, stacked offering/compliance/settlement/security surfaces, crypto/securities/payment/custody/tax/AML/privacy/security/authorization warn-and-proceed notice, safe crypto/wallet/security/privacy/evidence/contact links, and absence of horizontal overflow or fake sale stages, token value, prices, supply, allocations, vesting, audits, participants, investment opportunity, payments, wallet custody, chain settlement, or legal-compliance claims.
