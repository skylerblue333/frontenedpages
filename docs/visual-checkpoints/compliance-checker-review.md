# ComplianceChecker visual checkpoint

ComplianceChecker is not connected to a control catalog, jurisdictional rules, evidence store, test framework, policy owner, legal review, certification body, or attestation workflow. It cannot determine whether a requirement applies, a control operates effectively, a jurisdiction is covered, or a certification or filing exists. No control, policy, procedure, owner, scope, frequency, operating result, test, sample, exception, remediation, evidence file, audit, certification, attestation, jurisdictional conclusion, regulatory status, legal advice, or compliance score is displayed or asserted.

Files: `compliance-checker-desktop.webp`, `compliance-checker-mobile.png`.

The 390px capture was reviewed for readable wrapped assessment-unavailable disclosure, stacked no-control, no-evidence, and no-legal-claim cards, visible compliance/audit/contact links, legal disclaimer, sensitive-evidence warning, and absence of horizontal overflow.
