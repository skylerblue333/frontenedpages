# CreatorSpotlight visual checkpoint

CreatorSpotlight was upgraded from fabricated featured creator profiles, live status, verification, tiers, followers, subscribers, views, earnings, content previews, platform totals, search and filter behavior, follow and subscribe mutations, and promotional claims. It now presents an explicit discovery-readiness boundary because no verified profile, social graph, content, entitlement, payment, moderation, or analytics integration is connected.

No creator name, handle, avatar, biography, category, tier, verified badge, follower, subscriber, post, view, live viewer, ranking record, total creator count, subscriber count, earnings, content count, engagement, audience, growth, live-stream count, search, ranking, match, recommendation, follow, unfollow, subscribe, checkout, payment, content unlock, notification, live stream, share, identity, social graph, private content, entitlement, moderation decision, consent, payout, or audit record is read, generated, displayed, asserted, or simulated.

Files: `creator-spotlight-desktop.webp`, `creator-spotlight-mobile.png`.

The 390px capture was reviewed for readable wrapped unavailable disclosure, stacked discovery-readiness cards, stacked creator/metric/action/privacy boundaries, safe network/profile/onboarding links, and absence of horizontal overflow.
