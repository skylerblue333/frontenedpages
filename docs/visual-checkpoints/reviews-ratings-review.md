# ReviewsRatings visual checkpoint

ReviewsRatings was hardened from a generic reviews-and-ratings shell with an Activate or Deactivate toggle but no review, rating, marketplace, moderation, privacy, safety, security, or authorization backend. The nonfunctional toggle was removed. The registered route is `/reviews-ratings`. No reviewer, author, subject, review, rating, average, user, purchase, transaction, moderation decision, AI result, safety outcome, or availability state is displayed, calculated, stored, transmitted, verified, or mutated.

The page documents reviewer, author, and subject scope; review and rating provenance; documented scale and methodology; purchase or eligibility context; consent and authenticity; published policy; moderation; AI limitations; appeals; corrections; privacy; safety; marketplace and financial sensitivity controls; auditability; accessibility; and least-privilege authorization.

Files: `reviews-ratings-desktop.webp`, `reviews-ratings-mobile.png`.
