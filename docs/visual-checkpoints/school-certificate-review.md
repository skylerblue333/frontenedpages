# SchoolCertificate visual checkpoint

SchoolCertificate was already hardened from a fabricated certificate for a named learner and course with score, date, instructor, executive, hash, on-chain permanence, explorer action, social sharing, XP, time spent, and recommendations. The page now truthfully states that certificate verification is not connected and that no issuer, learner, assessment, credential, blockchain, transaction, or verification contract is established.

The page documents issuer and learner identity, achievement criteria, assessment evidence, signatures and verification rules, revocation and expiration, privacy and accessible export, blockchain network/hash/token/ownership proof, and absence of sharing operations.
