# CompetitiveRadar visual checkpoint

CompetitiveRadar was upgraded from hard-coded competitor records for Discord, Twitch, Roblox, and OpenSea with fabricated market shares, growth rates, feature counts, sentiment scores, threat and opportunity lists, six-month trend charts, competitive positioning, feature comparisons, a #2 market position, a 45% growth rate, 24 tracked competitors, three threats, and strategic recommendations. No source provenance, observation dates, definitions, licensing, survey, telemetry, calculation, uncertainty analysis, or review evidence was connected. All charts, rankings, scores, insights, and action recommendations were removed rather than presenting invented competitive intelligence as fact. No competitor, market share, growth rate, user count, feature score, sentiment score, ranking, threat, opportunity, trend, position, forecast, AI differentiator, or strategic recommendation is read, calculated, charted, exported, cached, or simulated.

Files: `competitive-radar-desktop.webp`, `competitive-radar-mobile.png`.

The 390px capture was reviewed for readable wrapped competitive-analysis-unavailable disclosure, stacked competitor-source, market-methodology, threat/opportunity, and access/licensing/privacy/audit readiness cards, clear no-recommendation warning, and absence of horizontal overflow.
