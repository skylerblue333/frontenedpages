# CommunityCreate visual checkpoint

CommunityCreate was upgraded from a live form accepting name, slug, description, category, and community type, followed by a create mutation and optimistic “Community created” result. It also advertised public, private, SKY444 token-gated, and paid premium communities without connected identity, authorization, wallet, token contract, subscription, moderation, persistence, or audit systems. The form and mutation were removed rather than implying that a social, financial, governance, or wallet-gated record was created. No name, slug, description, category, community type, owner, member, role, permission, channel, moderation policy, governance rule, notification, or social record is accepted, validated, stored, or simulated.

Files: `community-create-desktop.webp`, `community-create-mobile.png`.

The 390px capture was reviewed for readable wrapped creation-unavailable disclosure, stacked identity/authorization, metadata/moderation, token/payment, and persistence/audit readiness cards, visible wallet/payment warning, finance disclaimer, and absence of horizontal overflow.
