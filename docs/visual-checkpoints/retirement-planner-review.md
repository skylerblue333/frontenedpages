# RetirementPlanner visual checkpoint

RetirementPlanner was hardened from an authenticated-looking shell with nonfunctional Sign In, New, Search, Settings, loading, and empty-data controls without a financial-planning backend. Those controls were removed. The registered route is `/retirement-planner`. No owner, household, account, balance, holding, contribution, withdrawal, return, projection, income, tax, currency, risk, suitability, investment recommendation, or availability state is displayed, calculated, stored, transmitted, verified, or mutated.

The page documents owner and household scope, account and ledger provenance, explicit inputs and assumptions, scenario methodology, inflation, fees, taxes, currency, volatility, uncertainty, suitability and risk disclosures, human review, privacy, security, auditability, correction, and least-privilege authorization. It does not provide financial advice or generate projections.
