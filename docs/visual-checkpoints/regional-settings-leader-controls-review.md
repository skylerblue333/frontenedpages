# Regional Leader Control Center visual checkpoint

Regional Settings was redesigned into an interactive regional leader control center. It includes eight accessible feature switches for community/social, marketplace/creator, SkySchool, AI, crypto/wallet, live media, notifications, and experiments. The switches are explicitly local draft controls; they do not persist, publish, change production entitlements, or affect users because no regional administration backend is connected. The Marketplace switch was toggled during verification and the interface correctly surfaced “Draft changes are local only” and “Draft / not published.”

The SKY4 wallet area contains an explicitly illustrative `40,000,000 SKY4` planning figure. It is labeled “Demo figure only — not a live wallet balance or available funds,” with wallet address unavailable, network/provider not verified, and transfers/approvals disabled. No custody, balance, transaction, or funds claim is made.

The daily regional updates section provides a local draft preference only; the provider and daily source feed are unavailable. Decision-support guardrails require public, dated, independently verifiable information and explicitly prohibit collecting, revealing, ranking, or acting on material non-public information or insider-trading opportunities. No trading signal, investment recommendation, market advantage, or financial outcome is provided.
