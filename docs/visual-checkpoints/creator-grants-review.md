# CreatorGrants visual checkpoint

CreatorGrants was upgraded from fabricated fully-functional live-data and real-time-update claims, generic non-functional actions, and invented active-user, transaction, success-rate, and response-time metrics. It now presents an explicit grant-readiness boundary. No verified grant source, eligibility service, application workflow, review process, payment provider, or audit integration is connected.

No grantor, program, eligibility rule, deadline, applicant, creator, award, amount, availability, success rate, application, proposal, submission, review, selection, contract, payment, disbursement, reporting, repayment, appeal, grant total, award total, creator count, approval rate, funding result, or financial recommendation is read, displayed, calculated, initiated, asserted, or simulated.

Files: `creator-grants-desktop.webp`, `creator-grants-mobile.png`.

The 390px capture was reviewed for readable wrapped unavailable disclosure, stacked grant-readiness cards, stacked listing/application/metric/legal boundaries, financial warn-and-proceed notice, safe creator and availability links, and absence of horizontal overflow.
