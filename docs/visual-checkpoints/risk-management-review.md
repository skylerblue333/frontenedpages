# RiskManagement visual checkpoint

RiskManagement was hardened from a page claiming fully functional risk management with live data and real-time updates. It displayed generic feature cards and fabricated active users, transaction totals, success rate, and response time. Those claims and nonfunctional CTAs were removed. The registered route is `/risk-management`. No owner, organization, portfolio, holding, liability, price, exposure, limit, alert, risk score, forecast, recommendation, transaction, user, or availability state is displayed, calculated, stored, transmitted, verified, or mutated.

The page documents owner and organization scope, exposure and limit provenance, prices and currencies, concentration and liquidity, policy limits, freshness and reconciliation, transparent methodology, stress assumptions, uncertainty, model governance, alert ownership, human review, separate execution, custody, fees, taxes, privacy, security, compliance, auditability, and least-privilege authorization.

Files: `risk-management-desktop.webp`, `risk-management-mobile.png`.
