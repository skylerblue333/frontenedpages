# SSO visual checkpoint

SSO was hardened from an authenticated-looking identity-federation CRUD shell with nonfunctional Sign In, New, Search, Settings, loading, and empty-data controls without an identity provider or federation service. Those controls were removed. No provider, tenant, directory, user, authentication result, role, group, certificate, session, credential, security, privacy, compliance, or availability state is displayed, calculated, stored, transmitted, verified, granted, or mutated.

The page documents verified issuer and tenant scope, protocol metadata, redirect and audience policy, signing keys and certificate provenance, issuer and nonce/state validation, session lifecycle, logout and revocation, claim minimization, least-privilege roles, group ownership, provisioning/deprovisioning, MFA policy, rate limits, incident response, privacy, retention, auditability, and authorization. Files: `sso-desktop.webp`, `sso-mobile.png`.
