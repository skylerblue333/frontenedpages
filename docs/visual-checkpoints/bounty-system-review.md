# BountySystem visual checkpoint

BountySystem was upgraded from fabricated translation tasks and financial-incentive claims. The previous screen presented translation tasks with dollar rewards, deadlines, language qualifications, Accept buttons, a completed-bounty total, total earned, average rating, current streak, XP, feedback, and a translation-submission dialog. It claimed six available tasks, including legal and marketing translation, and showed fabricated values such as 23 completed bounties, $1,850 earned, a 4.8 rating, 12 days, and rewards from $40 to $200. No verified task owner, contributor identity, source content, rights and confidentiality terms, review process, payout provider, tax treatment, wallet integration, or audit contract was connected. Those tasks, balances, ratings, rewards, sample text, Accept controls, and submission actions were removed rather than implying work, payment, or financial entitlement.

Files: `bounty-system-desktop.webp`, `bounty-system-mobile.png`.

The 390px capture was reviewed for readable wrapped bounty disclosure, stacked task/content/reward/review cards, visible translation/payment/privacy links, financial and sensitive-data warning, and absence of horizontal overflow.
