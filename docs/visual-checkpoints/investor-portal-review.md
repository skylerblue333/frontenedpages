# InvestorPortal visual checkpoint

InvestorPortal was hardened from a screen that asserted a live ICO pre-sale and displayed token prices, bonuses, supply, raised amounts, investor counts, token balances, platform KPIs, tokenomics, roadmap completion, purchase widgets, Stripe and KYC claims, referral rewards, leaderboards, vesting and claim actions, and legal documents without verified issuer identity, securities terms, custody, payment, KYC/AML, tax, disclosure, privacy, or authorization controls. Those claims, live queries, purchase mutations, and unsupported actions were removed. No offer, token, balance, purchase, transaction, custody, investor record, or downloadable legal document is displayed or initiated from this page. The registered route is `/investor-portal`.

No auth check, investor lookup, ICO or token query, balance calculation, price or bonus calculation, purchase, referral, leaderboard, vesting claim, wallet connection, payment, transaction signing, settlement, refund, KYC/AML operation, API request, database read or write, notification, export, deletion, or personal-data operation is read, calculated, displayed, asserted, initiated, or simulated.

Files: `investor-portal-desktop.webp`, `investor-portal-mobile.png`.

The 390px capture was reviewed for readable wrapped unavailable disclosure, stacked investor-service cards, stacked eligibility/token-sale/purchase/custody/reporting/legal/privacy surfaces, financial/securities/crypto/custody/tax/KYC/AML/privacy/authorization warn-and-proceed notice, safe pitch/metrics/financial/wallet/KYC/privacy/security links, and absence of horizontal overflow or fake offers, token sales, investments, balances, purchases, vesting, transactions, liquidity, investor rights, payment, custody, or KYC/AML claims.
