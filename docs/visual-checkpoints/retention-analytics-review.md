# RetentionAnalytics visual checkpoint

RetentionAnalytics was hardened from an authenticated-looking shell with nonfunctional Sign In, New, Search, Settings, loading, and empty-data controls without an analytics backend. Those controls were removed. The registered route is `/retention-analytics`. No operator, organization, cohort, user, retention rate, churn rate, revenue, payment, forecast, AI result, or availability state is displayed, calculated, stored, transmitted, verified, or mutated.

The page documents cohort entry and observation windows, activity and churn definitions, denominators and censoring, exclusions, event and revenue provenance, attribution, refunds and tax treatment, privacy-preserving aggregation, timestamps and freshness, uncertainty, model identity and limits, fairness, security, retention, auditability, and least-privilege authorization.

Files: `retention-analytics-desktop.webp`, `retention-analytics-mobile.png`.
