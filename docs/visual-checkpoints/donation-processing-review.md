# DonationProcessing visual checkpoint

DonationProcessing already presents a truthful donation-readiness boundary. Donation processing is not enabled in this deployment. No campaign, beneficiary, donation, payment, receipt, refund, or payout result is reported, and the page does not verify a campaign or beneficiary, collect a donation, authorize or capture a payment, issue a receipt, process a refund, or disburse funds.

No campaign, beneficiary, cause, terms, donor identity, goal, deadline, restricted-fund policy, payment method, donation amount, authorization, capture, refund, chargeback, fee, donor total, receipt, tax output, disbursement, beneficiary payout, audit record, confirmation, settlement evidence, or charitable or financial outcome is read, calculated, displayed, asserted, initiated, or simulated.

Files: `donation-processing-desktop.webp`, `donation-processing-mobile.png`.

The 390px capture was reviewed for readable wrapped unavailable disclosure, stacked campaign/payment/settlement boundaries, safe fundraiser/payment/documentation links, current-state cards, sensitive-payment and beneficiary-credential warning, and absence of horizontal overflow.
