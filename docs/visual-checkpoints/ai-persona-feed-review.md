# AIPersonaFeed visual checkpoint

AIPersonaFeed was upgraded from blended AI/real-user feed queries, persona records, generated posts, topic filters, blend scores, likes, comments, shares, aggregate stats, and Seed/Generate Posts mutations to a truthful unavailable content boundary. It now states that no verified persona registry, content source, provenance, model, moderation policy, consent boundary, social graph, engagement store, or audit contract is connected. No synthetic or user content is presented as real.

Files: `ai-persona-feed-desktop.webp`, `ai-persona-feed-mobile.png`.
