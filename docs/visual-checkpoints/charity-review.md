# Charity visual checkpoint

Charity was upgraded from fabricated on-chain giving, donor, DAO, and impact claims. The previous screen presented “Transparent Giving — On-Chain” and claimed support for causes, DAO voting on fund allocation, real-world impact tracking, and full transparency. It exposed Campaigns, DAO Voting, Leaderboard, and Impact tabs, donation dialogs, voting controls, and live campaign statistics. It also contained fabricated proposal amounts and vote counts, donor names and rankings, SKY444 donation amounts, beneficiary and partner claims, “100% of donations” and “on-chain verified” assurances, and mutation paths for donations. No verified charity registration, beneficiary identity, campaign owner, due diligence, wallet or payment provider, custody model, receipt, DAO contract, vote weight, execution policy, impact methodology, tax treatment, or audit record was connected. Those claims and actions were removed rather than implying charitable or financial outcomes.

Files: `charity-desktop.webp`, `charity-mobile.png`.

The 390px capture was reviewed for readable wrapped charitable-integration disclosure, stacked charity/wallet/DAO/impact cards, visible wallet/payment/audit links, financial and sensitive-data warning, and absence of horizontal overflow.
