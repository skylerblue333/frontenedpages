# HIPAA visual checkpoint

HIPAA was hardened from a sign-in path and generic new, settings, search, loading, and empty-state controls without a defined covered-entity or business-associate role, PHI inventory, risk analysis, safeguards, agreements, audit evidence, breach response, patient-rights process, or authorization boundary. Those unsupported controls were removed. The actual registered route is `/h-i-p-a-a`; `/hipaa` returns 404 and was not indexed as the checkpoint route.

This page does not claim HIPAA compliance or certification. No auth check, covered-entity determination, PHI lookup, patient lookup, risk calculation, safeguard verification, audit lookup, breach assessment, agreement generation, API request, database read or write, notification, export, deletion, or personal-data operation is read, calculated, displayed, asserted, initiated, or simulated.

Files: `hipaa-desktop.webp`, `hipaa-mobile.png`.

The 390px capture was reviewed for readable wrapped unavailable disclosure, stacked healthcare-compliance cards, stacked PHI/safeguards/risk/contracts surfaces, healthcare/privacy/security/legal/authorization warn-and-proceed notice, safe privacy/security/data-rights/identity/evidence links, and absence of horizontal overflow or fake PHI, patient, safeguard, risk, breach, certification, compliance, or legal-sufficiency claims.
