# RevenueTracking visual checkpoint

RevenueTracking was hardened from a page claiming fully functional live revenue tracking and real-time updates. It displayed generic feature cards and fabricated active users, transaction totals, a success rate, and response time. Those claims and nonfunctional CTAs were removed. The registered route is `/revenue-tracking`. No owner, organization, user, transaction, revenue, payment, amount, currency, fee, refund, tax, success rate, response time, forecast, AI result, or availability state is displayed, calculated, stored, transmitted, verified, or mutated.

The page documents authenticated scope, payment and accounting provenance, gross/net and currency semantics, fees, refunds, tax, recognition, attribution, reconciliation, cohort and denominator definitions, deduplication, suppression, freshness, uncertainty, model limits, privacy, security, compliance, auditability, and least-privilege authorization.
