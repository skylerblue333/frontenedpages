# ChatBot visual checkpoint

ChatBot is registered at `/chat-bot` (not `/chatbot`). Chatbot services are not connected in this deployment. The previous screen exposed a sign-in gate, New action, search, settings, loading state, and empty-item prompt, but no verified chatbot or message contract was connected. Those controls were removed because they could imply conversations, history, persistence, or successful AI access that cannot be proven. No provider, model, version, response, latency, capability, availability, message, conversation, search result, memory, personalization, retention behavior, or privacy guarantee is simulated.

Files: `chatbot-desktop.webp`, `chatbot-mobile.png`.

The 390px capture was reviewed for readable wrapped unavailable disclosure, stacked model/history/privacy readiness cards, visible safe status links, sensitive-data warning, and absence of horizontal overflow. `/chatbot` remains a 404 alias; the registered route is `/chat-bot`.
