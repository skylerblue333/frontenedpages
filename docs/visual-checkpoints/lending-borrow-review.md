# LendingBorrow visual checkpoint

LendingBorrow was hardened from a screen that claimed a fully functional lending service with live data and real-time updates, presented feature promises and inert action buttons, and fabricated active users, transaction totals, success rate, and response time without lender or borrower identity, jurisdiction, offer terms, rates, collateral, custody, credit decisions, repayment, liquidation, disclosures, privacy, fraud, or authorization controls. Those claims and controls were removed. No offer, loan, balance, rate, transaction, collateral, repayment, liquidation, or financial outcome is displayed or initiated from this page. The registered route is `/lending-borrow`.

No auth check, lender or borrower query, offer calculation, credit decision, rate quote, wallet connection, custody, disbursement, repayment, liquidation, transaction submission, search, API request, database read or write, notification, export, deletion, or financial-data operation is read, calculated, displayed, asserted, initiated, or simulated.

Files: `lending-borrow-desktop.webp`, `lending-borrow-mobile.png`.

The 390px capture was reviewed for readable wrapped unavailable disclosure, stacked lending-service cards, stacked party/terms/custody/privacy surfaces, financial/crypto/legal/credit/privacy/fraud/security/authorization warn-and-proceed notice, safe crypto/custody/financial-planning/identity/legal links, and absence of horizontal overflow or fake offer, loan, balance, rate, transaction, collateral, repayment, liquidation, financial outcome, custody, credit decision, guaranteed-return, or lending-service claims.
