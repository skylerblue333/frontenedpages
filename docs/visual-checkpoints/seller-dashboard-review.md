# SellerDashboard visual checkpoint

SellerDashboard was hardened from a generic authenticated CRUD shell with unsupported Sign In, New, Search, Settings, loading, and empty-data behavior. No seller, catalog, order, inventory, revenue, customer, rating, payout, tax, verification, payment, privacy, or authorization backend was established. Those controls and claims were removed.

The page documents seller ownership/authorization, legal entity and identity/KYC, marketplace permissions, product and inventory provenance, pricing/availability, orders/fulfillment, returns/disputes, tax rules, source records, periods/currency, fees/refunds/attribution, ratings/reviews, payment provider, settlement/reconciliation, payout destination, holds/fraud review, tax documents, audit history, privacy, security, and honest operational states. Files: `seller-dashboard-desktop.webp`, `seller-dashboard-mobile.png`.
