# FundraiserTools visual checkpoint

FundraiserTools already contained a truthful disabled fundraising boundary and was verified without additional feature claims. Fundraising services are not enabled in this deployment; no campaign, beneficiary, donation, goal, receipt, payout, payment, tax, donor, or personal-data result is reported.

The screen does not create or verify campaigns, beneficiaries, donations, payments, goals, donor records, tax receipts, refunds, disbursements, or payouts, and cannot attest that funds were collected, safeguarded, transferred, or used for a stated purpose. No charitable, financial, crypto, or donor outcome should be inferred.

Files: `fundraiser-tools-desktop.webp`, `fundraiser-tools-mobile.png`.

The 390px capture was reviewed for readable wrapped unavailable disclosure, stacked fundraising boundary cards, current-state cards, finance/crypto/tax/privacy/safety warn-and-proceed notice, safe payment/documentation/contact links, and absence of horizontal overflow.
