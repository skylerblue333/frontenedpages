# SearchHistory visual checkpoint

SearchHistory was hardened from a placeholder with a nonfunctional Activate/Deactivate control and no query-history, account, retention, or deletion backend. That control was removed. No query, timestamp, owner, tenant, device, personalization, retention, deletion, user, privacy, compliance, or availability state is displayed, calculated, stored, transmitted, verified, granted, or mutated.

The page documents authenticated owner and tenant scope, query provenance, timestamps and timezone, device/session boundaries, sensitive-term handling, purpose limitation, retention and deletion authority, propagation and legal hold semantics, consent and opt-out, personalization boundaries, encryption, least-privilege access, abuse controls, auditability, incident response, and user-visible controls. Files: `search-history-desktop.webp`, `search-history-mobile.png`.
