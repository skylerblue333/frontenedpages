# AssetTracking visual checkpoint

AssetTracking was already hardened to a truthful unavailable asset-data boundary. The page does not connect to a portfolio, custody, blockchain, market-data, inventory, or accounting source. It cannot prove that an asset is held, calculate a value or return, verify ownership, or record a transfer. No asset type, identifier, account, wallet, custodian, inventory record, balance, price, market value, cost basis, return, performance, liquidity, inventory quantity, custody proof, transfer history, reconciliation, audit event, authorization, or settlement evidence is displayed or available.

Files: `asset-tracking-desktop.webp`, `asset-tracking-mobile.png`.

The 390px capture was reviewed for readable wrapped asset-data disclosure, stacked registry/valuation/ownership cards, visible portfolio/crypto/documentation links, custody warning, and absence of horizontal overflow.
