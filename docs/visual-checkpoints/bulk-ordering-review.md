# BulkOrdering visual checkpoint

BulkOrdering was already hardened to a truthful unavailable bulk-commerce boundary. The page does not calculate bulk pricing, reserve inventory, authorize payment, create an order, generate a quote, schedule shipping, or claim fulfillment. No trusted catalog, quantity or customer-specific pricing, minimum-order rules, tax and shipping calculation, inventory reservation, quote expiry, payment authorization, idempotent order creation, procurement approval, cancellation and refund handling, or shipment and fulfillment tracking is connected. No product, quantity, price tier, discount, minimum order, tax, shipping, quote, expiration, stock, allocation, reservation, procurement approval, warehouse, shipment, delivery, payment, order, receipt, refund, cancellation, or fulfillment evidence is displayed or created.

Files: `bulk-ordering-desktop.webp`, `bulk-ordering-mobile.png`.

The 390px capture was reviewed for readable wrapped commerce disclosure, stacked quote/supply/transaction cards, visible order/checkout/documentation links, payment and shipping warning, and absence of horizontal overflow.
