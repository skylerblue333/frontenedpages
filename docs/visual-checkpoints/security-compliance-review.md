# SecurityCompliance visual checkpoint

SecurityCompliance was hardened from a fabricated “Phase 32” live-control dashboard that claimed identity hardening, fraud detection, AI abuse filtering, fixed rate limits, transaction safety, tamper-proof logs, privacy protection, trust scoring, circuit breakers, compliance readiness, “All Systems Secure,” blocked/throttled/allowed counts, a trust score, hard-coded audit events, and payment-linked trust consequences. Those claims and controls were removed.

The page documents independently evidenced controls, identity and authorization, secrets/keys, encryption, rate limiting, fraud and abuse processes, logging, incident response, applicable obligations, scope, policy versions, control mapping, assessor independence, exceptions, privacy governance, retention/deletion, access requests, sensitive-data handling, and auditability. Files: `security-compliance-desktop.webp`, `security-compliance-mobile.png`.
