No course title, instructor, lesson count, duration, level, category, price, rating, student count, availability, enrollment, entitlement, payment, ownership, subscription, learner record, content-access decision, accreditation, outcome, certificate, or curriculum-quality claim is fabricated, created, changed, read, stored, or simulated.
