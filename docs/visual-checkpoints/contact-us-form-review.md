# ContactUsForm visual checkpoint

ContactUsForm was upgraded from a generic contact-us placeholder with an Activate/Deactivate toggle and no recipient, message fields, validation, delivery path, ticket system, response promise, privacy notice, or audit evidence. It has been removed rather than implying that a support request can be sent or will receive a response. No name, email, phone number, subject, message, attachment, ticket, case number, delivery receipt, response time, support status, escalation, or contact record is collected, stored, displayed, or simulated.

Files: `contact-us-form-desktop.webp`, `contact-us-form-mobile.png`.

The 390px capture was reviewed for readable wrapped support-contact-unavailable disclosure, stacked channel/recipient, message delivery/ticket, response/escalation, and privacy/audit readiness cards, explicit sensitive-data warning, and absence of horizontal overflow.
