# ComplianceCenter visual checkpoint

ComplianceCenter is not connected to verified KYC, consent, data-rights, audit, policy, or regulatory services. The page does not collect identity documents or sensitive personal data, calculate a compliance score or risk result, approve identity, record consent, submit an export or deletion request, provide a legal deadline, or claim that any regulatory obligation has been met. No document, identity, age, address, KYC level, risk score, approval, rejection, consent type, legal basis, version, timestamp, withdrawal, access, correction, export, deletion, retention, deadline, fulfillment, or regulatory result is collected, recorded, displayed, or asserted.

Files: `compliance-center-desktop.webp`, `compliance-center-mobile.png`.

The 390px capture was reviewed for readable wrapped compliance-unavailable disclosure, stacked identity, consent, and data-rights no-claim cards, visible security/audit/contact links, sensitive-data warning, and absence of horizontal overflow.
