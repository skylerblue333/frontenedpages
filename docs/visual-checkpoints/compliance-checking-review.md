# ComplianceChecking visual checkpoint

ComplianceChecking is not connected to a defined compliance framework, jurisdictional rule set, control owner, evidence repository, testing procedure, exception process, legal reviewer, auditor, or certification workflow. It cannot verify that a requirement applies, a control works, evidence is complete, or a finding was resolved. No framework, jurisdiction, requirement, period, applicability, legal interpretation, control test, sample, evidence item, exception, remediation, approval, pass, fail, score, certification, audit opinion, or compliance conclusion is displayed or asserted.

Files: `compliance-checking-desktop.webp`, `compliance-checking-mobile.png`.

The 390px capture was reviewed for readable wrapped verification-unavailable disclosure, stacked no-scope, no-evidence, and no-outcome-claim cards, visible checker/reporting/contact links, legal disclaimer, sensitive-evidence warning, and absence of horizontal overflow.
