# BrandGuidelines visual checkpoint

BrandGuidelines was already hardened to a truthful unavailable brand-governance boundary. The previous screen exposed an authenticated shell with New, Search, Settings, loading, and empty-item behavior, but no verified brand registry, asset store, ownership and license model, design-token source, version history, approval workflow, accessibility checker, distribution permission, or audit contract was connected. Those controls were removed because the screen cannot establish that a guideline or brand asset is current, authorized, or approved. No logo, color, typography, component, voice, spacing, icon, token, example, version, canonical standard, owner, license, usage restriction, approval, attribution, consent, publication permission, download authorization, asset package, export, email, social share, external delivery, or compliance decision is displayed, inferred, created, or sent.

Files: `brand-guidelines-desktop.webp`, `brand-guidelines-mobile.png`.

The 390px capture was reviewed for readable wrapped brand-governance disclosure, stacked guideline/rights/distribution cards, visible content/media/access/contact links, sensitive-data warning, and absence of horizontal overflow.
