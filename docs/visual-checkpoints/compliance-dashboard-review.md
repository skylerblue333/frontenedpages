# ComplianceDashboard visual checkpoint

ComplianceDashboard is not connected to an approved framework, control register, evidence store, finding workflow, remediation tracker, legal review, audit process, certification body, or reporting source of truth. It cannot show a compliance posture, determine a requirement, verify a control, track an issue, or claim that a report or certification is complete. No jurisdiction, framework, scope, control owner, reporting period, compliance percentage, score, control status, evidence freshness, certification readiness, finding, severity, exception, owner, remediation, due date, approval, or closure is displayed or inferred.

Files: `compliance-dashboard-desktop.webp`, `compliance-dashboard-mobile.png`.

The 390px capture was reviewed for readable wrapped reporting-unavailable disclosure, stacked no-framework, no-posture, and no-finding-claim cards, visible checker/audit/contact links, legal disclaimer, sensitive-reporting warning, and absence of horizontal overflow.
