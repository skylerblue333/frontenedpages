# AgeGate visual checkpoint

AgeGate was upgraded to remove an unsupported claim that the platform complied with 18 U.S.C. § 2257 and maintained performer records. The previous “Age Verified” state also overstated what a sessionStorage flag proves. The route now clearly labels continuation as a local adult-content self-attestation only; it does not claim identity, document, biometric, jurisdictional, legal, performer-age, or statutory compliance verification. The under-18 exit remains available, and Terms and Content Policy links are visible.

Files: `age-gate-desktop.webp`, `age-gate-mobile.png`.
