# SKY444CentralBank visual checkpoint

SKY444CentralBank was hardened from a live-looking monetary dashboard. It presented hardcoded token identities, max and circulating supplies, economy health, active-token counts, policy rates, staking APY, governance weight, treasury balances, protocol revenue, burn pools, allocation percentages, alerts, and nation-mode status as if verified. No ledger, market, custody, governance, accounting, or reporting service was connected. Those claims and controls were removed.

The page now provides a truthful monetary and treasury readiness boundary covering ledger/supply/reserve provenance, monetary policy and governance, treasury custody controls, market/risk/reporting evidence, secure approvals, reconciliation, auditability, privacy, incident response, and legal/regulatory review. No balance, price, yield, reserve, supply, transaction, policy, vote, treasury, or financial outcome is displayed, calculated, stored, transmitted, verified, granted, or mutated.

Finance warn-and-proceed language is present. Files: `sky444-central-bank-desktop.webp`, `sky444-central-bank-mobile.png`.
