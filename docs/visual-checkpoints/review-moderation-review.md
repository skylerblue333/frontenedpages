# ReviewModeration visual checkpoint

ReviewModeration was hardened from an authenticated-looking shell with nonfunctional Sign In, New, Search, Settings, loading, and empty-data controls without a review-moderation backend. Those controls were removed. The registered route is `/review-moderation`. No reviewer, author, subject, review, rating, transaction context, moderation decision, queue, AI result, safety outcome, or availability state is displayed, calculated, stored, transmitted, verified, or mutated.

The page documents reviewer, author, and subject scope; review and rating provenance; consent and authenticity; published policy and taxonomy; transparent criteria; AI model identity and uncertainty; human decision; appeal, escalation, correction, and safety response; privacy; security; retention; auditability; accessibility; and least-privilege authorization.
