# BookPage visual checkpoint

BookPage was upgraded from fabricated publication and commerce claims. The previous screen presented “The Chosen One” as a limited first edition, claimed seven chapters, twelve languages, 4,444 pre-orders, early-reader endorsements, a waitlist, signed digital copies, and an exclusive SKY444 airdrop. No verified manuscript, author record, chapter catalog, translation source, rights and licensing record, commerce provider, order ledger, email-consent system, delivery service, testimonial provenance, wallet integration, or audit contract was connected. Those claims and the Notify Me flow were removed rather than implying that a book exists for sale, has orders, or includes a token reward.

Files: `book-page-desktop.webp`, `book-page-mobile.png`.

The 390px capture was reviewed for readable wrapped publication disclosure, stacked title/rights/commerce/waitlist cards, visible publishing/media/privacy links, sensitive-data warning, and absence of horizontal overflow.
