# CommissionManagement visual checkpoint

CommissionManagement was upgraded from a sign-in gate, New action, search, settings, loading state, and empty-item prompt without a commission plan, sale attribution, earnings ledger, payout provider, payee authorization, tax policy, dispute workflow, or audit record. Those controls were removed rather than implying that a commission, earning, payout, balance, sale, or financial obligation exists. No commission rate, currency, sale, order, referral, seller, payee, earning, balance, payout, tax amount, fee, revenue, refund, dispute, or transaction status is read, calculated, displayed, stored, exported, or simulated.

Files: `commission-management-desktop.webp`, `commission-management-mobile.png`.

The 390px capture was reviewed for readable wrapped financial unavailable disclosure, stacked plan/rate, ledger/payout, payee/compliance, and search/audit readiness cards, visible finance disclaimer, sensitive-data warning, and absence of horizontal overflow.
