# ChatMVP visual checkpoint

ChatMVP is registered at `/chat-m-v-p` (not `/chat-mvp`). Chat, marketplace, payment, and AI-action services are not connected in this deployment. The previous screen contained mock conversation data, fabricated NOVA messages, online and unread indicators, designer matches with prices and delivery times, AI commands, tip and payment actions, service requests, listings, matching, earning options, calls, and optimistic success states. Those claims and mutations were removed because the underlying contracts, identity, payment, marketplace, and AI evidence were not verified. No conversation, person, presence, match, tip, payment, listing, balance, amount, recipient, service price, delivery estimate, transaction success, assistant response, external action, or recommendation is simulated.

Files: `chat-mvp-desktop.webp`, `chat-mvp-mobile.png`.

The 390px capture was reviewed for readable wrapped unavailability disclosure, stacked social/financial/AI claim boundaries, visible ChatBot/marketplace/wallet status links, sensitive-data and payment warning, and absence of horizontal overflow. `/chat-mvp` remains a 404 alias; the registered route is `/chat-m-v-p`.
