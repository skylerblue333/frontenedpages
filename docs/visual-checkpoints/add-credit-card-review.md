# AddCreditCard visual checkpoint

AddCreditCard was upgraded from a generic placeholder and inert Activate control to a payment-safe card-linking readiness boundary. It states that no payment processor, hosted-field or tokenization flow, card verification, consent, billing, authorization, receipt, fraud control, refund, or financial-data deletion contract is connected. The route does not collect or store card details and does not simulate activation.

Files: `add-credit-card-desktop.webp`, `add-credit-card-mobile.png`.

The 390px capture was reviewed for readable wrapped card-link disclosure, stacked provider/tokenization/authorization/data cards, visible payment/billing/contact links, payment-data safety warning, and absence of horizontal overflow.
