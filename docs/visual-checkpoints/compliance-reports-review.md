ComplianceReports was upgraded from claims of fully functional compliance reports with live data and real-time updates, advanced analytics, seamless automation, 802K+ active users, 2.4M total transactions, a 99.9% success rate, and 45ms average response time, with non-functional Get Started, Learn More, and Documentation buttons. No framework, jurisdiction, control register, evidence store, finding workflow, audit source, certification body, methodology, or reporting authority was connected. Those claims and actions were removed rather than presenting invented compliance evidence as a live report. No report, framework, jurisdiction, control, evidence, finding, severity, remediation, due date, approval, compliance percentage, score, certification, audit result, active-user count, transaction count, success rate, or response-time metric is read, calculated, displayed, generated, exported, cached, or simulated.

Files: `compliance-reports-desktop.webp`, `compliance-reports-mobile.png`.

The 390px capture was reviewed for readable wrapped report-unavailable disclosure, stacked framework/owner, controls/evidence, methodology/export, and audit/certification readiness cards, clear no-publication warning, sensitive-evidence warning, and absence of horizontal overflow.
